**Every candidate lands `over_budget`** — the direction flipped from pass 2's uniform
`under_budget_oversized`. This is the redesign working as intended: real net spend on the
fixture task clusters around 12,500–15,100 tokens regardless of knob wording (the genuine
content cost of reading three reference functions, matching the convention, editing, and
writing two tests), and **every tested ceiling so far (3,000–10,000) is smaller than that
real requirement**. `accuracy_rate` is still 0.0 for every candidate this pass, but for the
opposite reason pass 2 found — real signal, not floor noise, and the redesign's whole point
was to make that distinction visible.

## The move, taken via code

`optimizer.coordinate_ascent_step` on this data: `budget_margin` moves from `+1` to **`-1`** —
the loosest margin level ("padded generously... 2–3× the expected spend"), whose 10,000-token
ceiling comes closest to the real ~12,500–15,100 requirement (mean_loss 0.3377, an order of
magnitude better than every other candidate). No other knob moved — their neighbors were
evaluated against the stale `budget_margin=1` point (the same parallel-sweep gap pass 1 and 2
both had), and none beat the ~0.34 the accepted `budget_margin=-1` move already reached.

Final settings after pass 3: `budget_margin=-1, effort_tax=-1, calibration_aggressiveness=1,
calibration_decay=0, pass_b_feedback=1`.
